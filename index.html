Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2024

YouTube Shorts Shortcuts & Autoplay is a Chrome extension designed to enhance your YouTube Shorts viewing experience with keyboard shortcuts and autoplay features. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

NO DATA COLLECTION

We do NOT collect any personal information. Our extension operates entirely on your local device and does not:

  • Collect any personal data or user information
  • Track your browsing history or activities
  • Send any data to external servers
  • Use analytics, tracking tools, or cookies
  • Require user registration or accounts
  • Monitor your YouTube viewing habits

2. Data Storage

The extension stores only your preference settings locally on your device using Chrome's Storage API:

  • Autoplay settings: On/off status and playback interval
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Your custom shortcut preferences
  • Feature toggles: Loop play and other feature settings

Important: All data is stored locally in your browser and never transmitted elsewhere. You can delete this data at any time by removing the extension.

3. Permissions We Use

Our extension requires minimal permissions, each serving a specific functional purpose:

Required Permissions:

  • Storage: To save your settings and preferences locally on your device
  • Host permission (youtube.com): To interact with YouTube Shorts pages and implement shortcuts
  • Commands: To register and respond to keyboard shortcuts

These permissions are used solely to provide the extension's features and are never used to collect or transmit personal data.

4. Third-Party Services

This extension:

  • Does NOT communicate with any third-party services
  • Does NOT integrate with external APIs
  • Does NOT share data with advertisers or partners
  • Works entirely within your browser environment

5. Data Security

Your privacy and security are guaranteed through our architecture:

  • All operations occur locally on your device
  • No network requests are made to external servers
  • No data transmission means no risk of interception
  • Settings are protected by Chrome's built-in security

6. Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: View your settings through the extension's options page
  • Modification: Change your preferences at any time
  • Deletion: Remove all data by uninstalling the extension
  • Portability: Export/import settings if needed (feature dependent)

7. Children's Privacy

Our extension does not collect any data from users of any age. The extension is suitable for all ages as it merely provides interface enhancements for YouTube Shorts.

8. Changes to This Policy
